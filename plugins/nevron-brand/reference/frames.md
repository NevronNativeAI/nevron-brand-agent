# The frame (Okvir)

Read this before putting a frame on anything. The frame is the most misused
element in the system — it is not a decorative border, and the pattern variants
are not interchangeable wallpaper.

Source: `guidelines/Nevron_guidelines_01.pdf`, section **2.4** (2.4.1–2.4.6).
The assets: `plugins/nevron-brand/assets/frames/` — 19 approved patterns,
vendored from `J:/Produkcija/_Brand Identity/17_Concepts/Frames`.

## What it is

A pair of corner brackets — top-right and bottom-left — filled with a pattern.
Not solid bars. Do not hand-build one out of rectangles; inline the real asset.

- **2.4.1** The frame derives from the mark and shares its visual construction.
- **2.4.3** *"Mreža okvirja je enaka mreži monograma."* The frame's grid is the
  monogram's grid. Its proportions are not free.

## What it is for

**2.4.2** — *"Okvir služi za označevanje vidnih vsebin."*
**The frame marks content.** That is its whole job. It frames the thing you want
looked at: a cover, an image, a statement. A frame around nothing is the most
common mistake.

**2.4.4** — The slogan that goes with the frame is **"Let there be content"**.
That is why it closes the documents and the datasheets.

## The patterns mean something

**2.4.5** — the pattern variants exist to signal *"različnost in prilagodljivost
platform za vsebine"*: the variety and adaptability of the content platforms.
Diagonal, horizontal, vertical, thin, double, cross, X, full — a different
pattern reads as a different kind of content surface, not as a random skin.

Pick one deliberately, and keep it consistent within a single piece. The
datasheet builder picks at random for speed, but it prints which one it used so
the choice can be pinned.

## Where it belongs

**2.4.6** lists the sanctioned uses: a bag, a project cover page, a website
homepage, a notebook cover. All of them are **cover surfaces**.

In practice, for the skills in this plugin:

| Use | Verdict |
|---|---|
| Document and datasheet covers | Yes — the established use |
| Back cover with "Let there be content!" | Yes |
| A deck's title, section and closing slides | Yes |
| Framing one deliberate image or statement | Yes, sparingly — this is 2.4.2 |
| A small mark on every content slide | **No.** Repetition empties it |
| Pulled apart into a single bar or arm | **No.** That is not the shape |
| Behind body copy or a table | **No** |

## And the colour rule it sits inside

**2.2.1** — *"Nevron zagotavlja nevtralen okvir za prikaz vsebine, zato so barve
temu primerne."* Nevron is a neutral frame for someone else's content, and the
palette follows from that.

Restraint is intentional. But neutral is not the same as empty: a page still
needs structure, weight and something to look at. If a layout feels bare, the
answer is more content and stronger hierarchy — not more frame.
