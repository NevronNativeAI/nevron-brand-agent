02 — Abstract deck

The deck you give when the point is an idea

/nevron-brand:presentation-abstract

How Nevron sees the world, why a market is moving, what a partnership could be. Dark, spare, enormous type, one thought per slide.

You hand it

  • The argument — what the room should believe by the end
  • Hero imagery, or a decision to generate it

Not for feature, product or technical presentations. This deck has no template grid and no stat rows — it is one statement at a time.

03 — Full document

Cover, contents, numbered body

/nevron-brand:nevron-document

Reports, guides, specifications, proposals, manuals — anything needing a cover and a contents page. Matches the house Word template: grey cover with the brand frame and a topic icon, an introduction page carrying the legal disclaimer, an auto-numbered table of contents, then the body.

You hand it

  • Content, as Markdown or structured blocks
  • Title, date, topic icon
  • Images, tables, figures

--png also writes one PNG per page — the fastest way to check a layout without opening the PDF.

04 — Short document

Same engine, no front matter

/nevron-brand:nevron-document-nocover

Letters, legal documents, memos, meeting notes, checklists. Content starts on page one under the running header, and the back cover closes it. A contract does not want a cover page.

You hand it

  • The content, and who it is for
  • A running-header line — customer and subject

It has no builder of its own: it runs the nevron-document builder with the front matter switched off. So does service-report.

05 — Product datasheet

The InDesign house style, without InDesign

/nevron-brand:datasheet

Spec sheets for set-top boxes, servers and remotes: grey cover with a diagonal brand frame, a product overview with dimension lines, a ports band, and black-barred specification tables.

You hand it

  • Product name, model, positioning line
  • Specifications, as groups of rows
  • Product photography and a ports diagram

The cover frame is picked at random from the 19 approved patterns, and the build prints which one it used. Pin it with "frame": "FramesHorizontal.png".

06 — Service report

What an engineer leaves behind

/nevron-brand:service-report

A fixed skeleton — introduction, service details, scope of work, service delivery, testing and verification, conclusion and suggestions, signature — followed by a captioned photo attachments page.

You hand it

  • Customer, vessel or property, dates, engineer
  • What was done, section by section
  • Site photographs with captions

Read the previous report for that customer before writing a new one — the wording carries over, and the skill says so.

07 — Client offer

A priced ponudba as a page you send

/nevron-brand:offer

The page served at clients.nevron.co/<Hotel-Name>/ponudba: dark hero with a validity countdown, the lead module, what else the pilot includes, the timeline, and the licensing section. Plus a PDF of the same offer for email.

Two pricing modes

  • NevronCore pricelist — from the live rate card. Monthly is the daily rate × quantity × 30; capacity is the higher of beds or rooms; the bill has a floor.
  • Custom pricelist — you supply the lines and the totals.

The rate card is read live when the share is reachable, and from a vendored copy when it is not. The offer states which, and how fresh it is.

08 — Limits

What it will not do

  • Invent a brand asset. Missing icon or illustration? It says so.
  • Take assets from elsewhere on your disk. Finding a file is not permission.
  • Generate imagery unprompted — and never a photograph of a real named property.
  • Round a corner. Radius 0 is a hard rule.
  • Hide a gap. What it cannot reproduce faithfully, it warns about.
